    Most accurate: stand at the gate with your phone and press the button above. Or type a name and click the map where the gate is. Drag a gate to adjust it.

    Most accurate: stand at the office's entrance with your phone and press the button above; visitors are guided to this spot. Or pick an office and click its building on the map. Clicking again moves the pin.
